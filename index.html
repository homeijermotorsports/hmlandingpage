Meet the Team

Michael Homeijer

Lead Driver & Team Owner

Lead driver Michael brings a diverse motorsport background to the track, having started his career racing Slingshots before working in NASCAR and later expanding into drifting and time attack. Now, he is channeling that lifelong passion into launching the foundation of what will soon grow into a multi-car race team, Homeijer Motorsports.

Emily Crisafulli

Head of Marketing & Partnerships

Emily brings a strong background in marketing and branding, backed by years of experience in driving growth and building strategic partnerships. She is now applying that expertise to help build and expand the foundation of Homeijer Motorsports, while developing a network of like-minded brands interested in growing together.

Our Team Goal

Homeijer Motorsports is built on a foundation of diverse racing disciplines, bringing together knowledge and experience from different styles and industries to build a collaborative team of drivers eager to learn from one another. Rather than chasing vanity metrics and fleeting social media views, we are focused on growing through shared expertise and meaningful, mutually beneficial partnerships. We look to align with companies that share our drive and values, going beyond simple online visibility by actively advocating for our partners, making in-person appearances, and sharing the true value of their brand across a wide range of communities.

2026 Season Recap

Reflections from the 2026 Season

Even with years of racing experience, our 2026 entry into GRIDLIFE’s ClubTR class came together pretty last minute, turning the season into a major learning experience. Because we didn't have much time to plan ahead, we spent a lot of the year reacting on the fly, though we still made the most of what we had. Connecting with other drivers at each event gave us a better understanding of the tracks, car setup, and how to approach each weekend. We are taking all of that knowledge into 2027 as we roll out major engine, weight, and aero upgrades.

Just as important as our time on track were the in-person events and networking we did with our partners. Growing our audiences together and building long-term support systems off the track helped shape how we plan to operate moving forward. Starting from the ground up on short notice wasn't easy, and it definitely brought plenty of stress and long days, but that experience is the exact reason we are ready to take on 2027 with a whole new mindset.
